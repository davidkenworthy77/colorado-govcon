MMGY destination study, 2026.

Same model, two characters. Category mode is a salesperson: sells you a shortlist, hunts best-of lists on nine of ten questions, leans on awards and proof, 97% positive, almost incapable of criticism. Named mode is an investigator: checks the specifics, goes to official and primary sources, wants cost, conditions, logistics, problems. 80% positive, will surface negatives.

Say: the negatives in named mode are always the same. Cost, crowds, traffic, noise, recovery. Never the product. Every one of them is answerable with a page nobody has written. Most DMO sites are written entirely for the salesperson.
